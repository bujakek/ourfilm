A wedding photo sharing app should solve one simple problem: your guests take
hundreds of photos, but most of them never reach you. OurFilm gives everyone one
private event camera, opened through a QR code or link, so those candid moments
become part of the same story.

It is not another group chat and it is not an open upload folder. Each guest
gets a fixed roll of shots and takes photos inside the event camera. There is no
app to install, no guest account to create and no camera-roll upload to explain.

## How OurFilm works

1. Create your wedding film and choose when the camera closes.
2. Give each guest 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 shots.
3. Choose whether photos appear immediately or when the event ends.
4. Share the event QR code or private link.
5. Guests enter their name and start shooting in their mobile browser.
6. Download the finished gallery as a ZIP after the wedding.

There is no preview and no retake. Guests press the shutter, use one frame and
move on. That small constraint is what makes the experience feel closer to a
disposable camera than a shared cloud folder.

## Why couples use a guest photo app

A professional photographer captures the ceremony, portraits and the moments
that cannot be missed. Guests see something different: the table jokes, the
quiet reactions, the journey to the venue and the dance floor from inside the
crowd.

The two sets of photos complement each other. A guest camera is not a cheaper
replacement for a photographer. It is a way to keep the parts of the day that
only your friends and family could see.

## One QR code instead of several group chats

| Group chats and folders                     | OurFilm                                          |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Guests must remember to send photos later   | Photos join the event while they are taken       |
| Images get buried between messages          | Every ready photo belongs to one private gallery |
| Access rules vary by app and account        | Guests join from a QR code or link               |
| Unlimited snapping becomes background noise | Every person gets a chosen roll of shots         |

The QR code can be printed on table cards, placed near the bar or shared in the
wedding group before the day. The important part is timing: guests should meet
the camera while the event is happening, not in a reminder sent a week later.

## Private by default

Each OurFilm event has a private, unindexed address. The photo storage is not a
public folder, and the host can decide whether guests are allowed to see the
gallery. Unwanted photos can be hidden before the final album is shared.

No guest registration is required. A guest gives a display name so their roll
and photos can be identified inside that event; they do not need to create an
OurFilm account.

## Photos worth keeping

OurFilm keeps high-resolution, print-ready versions for the final download. It
does not claim to preserve untouched camera originals: images are prepared for
reliable venue uploads while retaining enough detail for prints.

The couple can download the complete album in one ZIP file instead of saving
photos individually from messages.

## Free for a small test event

An event can be used free with up to five participants. Every participant still
gets the roll length chosen by the host. The one-time event upgrade removes the
participant cap, but it does not remove the per-person shot limit — the finite
roll is part of the format.

There is no subscription and guests are never asked to pay.

## Is OurFilm the right kind of photo sharing app?

Choose OurFilm when you want:

- a browser-based guest camera rather than an app download;
- a limited number of intentional shots per guest;
- an immediate or end-of-event reveal;
- a private gallery and one complete download;
- a simple experience focused on photos.

Choose a broader event platform if you need video, an audio guestbook, RSVP,
live slideshows, camera-roll uploads or visual film filters. Those features are
not part of the current OurFilm experience.
