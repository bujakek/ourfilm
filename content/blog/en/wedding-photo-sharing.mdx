Your photographer's images arrive six weeks after the wedding, and they are
beautiful. Your guests' photos are taken that same evening — and usually stay
exactly where they were taken.

## Why the group chat is not enough

Most couples try a messaging group, and three things happen:

| What the chat does        | What you wanted         |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Compresses the image      | Print-ready resolution  |
| Buries it in conversation | One place, downloadable |
| Only reaches members      | Every guest, no sign-up |

Compression is the one that hurts. By the time a photo arrives, enough data is
gone to show on an A4 print.

## What works

One address everyone can reach and everyone can upload to. No account, no app —
a guest scans the code, picks their photos, and that is it.

Afterwards the couple downloads the lot as a single ZIP file.
