OurFilm is a Lense alternative for weddings that need a focused digital
disposable camera rather than a larger photo-sharing platform. Guests scan a QR
code, enter their name and take a finite number of photos directly in their
mobile browser.

**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a simple, capture-only wedding film.
Choose Lense when camera-roll uploads, filters, video, a live photo wall or
guest-count pricing are part of your plan.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, so it is not an independent review.
Lense features and public pricing were checked against its [wedding
page](https://lense.app/disposable-camera-app-for-weddings) and [pricing
page](https://lense.app/pricing) on 2026-08-29. Prices and inclusions may change.

## What Lense adds

Lense combines a disposable-camera experience with a wider shared-album tool.
Guests can capture new photos or add existing ones from their camera roll. The
service also offers visual effects, a live event display and an optional video
upgrade.

Its listed plans are based on guest count, from a free small event to packages
for hundreds of guests. The standard experience allows up to 25 captures per
person, with an unlimited-capture add-on available.

Lense is therefore the stronger option when flexibility matters more than a
strict finite-film format.

## What OurFilm keeps simple

OurFilm asks the host for only the rules needed by the camera:

- when the event ends;
- 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 shots per participant;
- immediate or end-of-event development;
- whether guests may see the gallery.

There are no visual filters, uploads from the phone library, videos, live
slideshows or unlimited-shot upgrades. Guests take photos in the moment, and
the host receives one private gallery with a full ZIP download.

## Pricing model differences

Lense prices by the number of guests. Its public pricing page currently lists
up to seven guests free, then multiple paid participant tiers. Unlimited
captures and video are separate one-time add-ons.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. One event purchase removes the
participant cap, but every person still has the chosen finite roll. That means
the paid plan changes how many people may join, not what kind of camera they
receive.

## When Lense is the better choice

Choose Lense if you need:

- photos already stored in guests' camera rolls;
- video capture or uploads;
- a live slideshow at the venue;
- vintage-style effects;
- optional unlimited captures;
- pricing matched to a known guest tier;
- a platform designed for repeat professional events.

## When OurFilm is the better choice

Choose OurFilm if you want:

- a stricter no-preview, no-retake wedding camera;
- a specific 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36-frame roll;
- a browser-only guest experience;
- a private, unindexed gallery;
- an immediate or event-end reveal;
- no extras beyond photography and the final download.

## Switching from Lense

OurFilm cannot import a Lense event or gallery. Download the existing album
before switching, then create a new event and replace every printed or digital
link. Test the new code at the size and distance guests will actually scan.
