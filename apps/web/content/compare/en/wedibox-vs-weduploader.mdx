**The short answer:** choose Wedibox when you want one wedding portal for media,
guest messages, the website, RSVPs and seating. Choose WedUploader when original
guest files should go directly into your own Google Drive and never depend on a
separate album expiry date.

Both services collect photos and videos from a QR code without a guest app.
Wedibox emphasises a complete guest-facing wedding hub; WedUploader emphasises
file ownership and a flexible Drive-backed album.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review.
Features and pricing models were checked against the official [Wedibox pricing
page](https://www.wedibox.com/pricing) and WedUploader [product
page](https://weduploader.com/) and [pricing
page](https://weduploader.com/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Prices may change.

## Wedibox vs WedUploader at a glance

| Feature          | Wedibox                            | WedUploader                                     |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Guest access     | QR code or link; no app or login   | QR code or link; no guest app or login          |
| Media            | Photos, videos and voice messages  | Photos, videos, captions and voicemails         |
| Primary storage  | Wedibox-hosted gallery             | Host's connected Google Drive                   |
| Album expiry     | Storage period depends on the plan | Albums advertised with no expiration date       |
| Live display     | Live slideshow                     | Fullscreen slideshow photo wall in Premium      |
| Wedding website  | Included on Wedding and All-In-One | Links to an existing wedding site or itinerary  |
| RSVP and seating | Detailed tools on All-In-One       | Seating chart sold as a Premium add-on          |
| Free option      | 50 guest photo uploads             | Core Drive upload service available free        |
| Paid model       | $29, $49 or $79 one-time plans     | One-time Premium upgrade; live price is dynamic |

## Choose Wedibox for an all-in-one guest portal

Wedibox combines unlimited media uploads on paid plans with a wedding website,
digital and audio guestbooks, a live slideshow and original-quality ZIP export.
Its All-In-One package adds RSVP details, meal choices, plus-ones, seating,
music and registry links.

That consolidation is valuable when the couple has not yet chosen separate
website and guest-management tools. The Embed plan also offers a smaller route
for adding a gallery to an existing site.

## Choose WedUploader for direct file ownership

WedUploader sends original-quality uploads into the host's connected Google
Drive. It advertises unlimited guests, uploads and albums, with the available
capacity ultimately depending on the Drive account.

Premium adds a real-time gallery, captions, private voicemails, layouts,
colours, a personalised URL, event managers, printable QR templates and a
fullscreen slideshow. Couples can also decide which images appear publicly in
the gallery while keeping other uploads private.

## Pricing and long-term storage

Wedibox publishes three one-time paid options: Embed, Wedding and All-In-One.
Their upload windows range from three months to one year, with one or two years
of gallery storage depending on the package.

WedUploader offers free core uploading and a one-time Premium upgrade. Its live
price is rendered dynamically, so confirm the checkout rather than relying on
an older fixed-dollar comparison. Because the original files live in Google
Drive, the host must also have enough Drive storage for the expected media.

## The deciding question

Choose Wedibox if one polished wedding destination should replace several
planning tools. Choose WedUploader if direct ownership of original files and a
non-expiring Drive-backed album matter more.

If you do not need a planning portal or Drive ingestion and prefer guests to
take a limited set of new pictures, consider a
[camera-first wedding photo experience](/en/digital-disposable-camera-for-weddings).
