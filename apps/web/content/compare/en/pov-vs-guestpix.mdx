**The short answer:** choose POV when the wedding activity should feel like a
custom digital disposable camera. Choose GuestPix when the priority is
collecting as many guest photos, videos and messages as possible in an
organised shared gallery.

Both services use a QR code and avoid a full guest app download. POV shapes how
people take pictures; GuestPix shapes how media is collected, organised and
shared.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review.
Features and plan details were checked against the official POV [wedding
page](https://pov.camera/use-cases/weddings) and [pricing
calculator](https://pov.camera/pricing), plus the [GuestPix wedding pricing
page](https://guestpix.com/weddings-pricing/), on 2026-09-17. Plans may change.

## POV vs GuestPix at a glance

| Feature             | POV                                       | GuestPix                                |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Core experience     | Custom digital disposable camera          | Shared wedding media gallery            |
| Guest entry         | QR/link through App Clips or Instant Apps | QR code or private link in the browser  |
| Shot limit          | Host chooses the guest limit              | No equivalent creative per-person limit |
| Reveal              | Host chooses when photos reveal           | Live gallery and photo/video slideshow  |
| Camera-roll uploads | Yes                                       | Yes                                     |
| Video               | Not a central wedding feature             | Yes                                     |
| Guestbook           | Not the primary format                    | Written and video guestbook             |
| Physical products   | Optional NFC block and photobook          | Printable QR templates                  |
| Free use            | Up to 10 guests                           | Up to 50 photo uploads                  |
| Paid model          | Guest-count calculator                    | One-time wedding packages               |

## Choose POV for a camera-led event

POV lets the host choose a visual style, cover, number of pictures per guest
and reveal timing. Guests can capture inside POV or upload from their camera
roll. App Clips and Instant Apps provide a lightweight, app-like entry without
the full installation.

Styled QR templates, a physical NFC block and an optional photobook extend the
camera into the wedding design and the post-event keepsake. POV is the stronger
choice when the act of shooting should have rules and a distinct visual layer.

## Choose GuestPix for broad media collection

GuestPix paid plans support unlimited photos and videos subject to fair-use
terms, moderation, a live slideshow, written and video guestbooks, likes,
comments and multiple album organisation. Guests use the browser and do not
need an account for the standard contribution flow.

The longer upload windows are useful when guests will select and add their best
files after the wedding. GuestPix is the stronger option when completeness and
organisation matter more than limiting each person's camera.

## Pricing structure

POV prices personal events by guest count and offers up to ten guests free. Its
business option can add custom branding, a live slideshow and support.

GuestPix offers a free 50-photo gallery and several one-time wedding packages.
Paid tiers vary by visual customisation, album structure and upload/hosting
window. Prices can display in several currencies, so compare the current total
for the features you actually need.

## The deciding question

Choose POV if you want to design the camera and its reveal. Choose GuestPix if
you want a wider photo, video and guestbook archive with more gallery features.

If the disposable-camera rule matters but POV's extra products do not, compare
a [simpler finite-roll guest camera](/en/digital-disposable-camera-for-weddings)
before deciding.
