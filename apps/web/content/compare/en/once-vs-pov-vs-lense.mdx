**The short answer:** Once is strongest for a styled film with flexible
development and voice memories; POV is strongest for guest-entry technology,
event customisation and physical products; Lense is strongest for a live photo
wall, camera-roll uploads and optional video.

All three recreate the disposable-camera idea on guests' phones. They let a
host shape the event and avoid requiring a full guest app, but their extra
features lead to three different experiences.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review.
Details were checked against the official Once [wedding](https://once.film/wedding)
and [pricing](https://once.film/pricing) pages, POV [wedding
page](https://pov.camera/use-cases/weddings) and [pricing
calculator](https://pov.camera/pricing), and Lense [pricing
page](https://lense.app/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Plans may change.

## Once: best for a developed film experience

Once gives hosts fully customisable rolls and a custom development schedule.
The film can stay private or be shared, and the official pricing page lists
unlimited memory storage and an optional unlimited-shots upgrade.

Its Voice Tapes product and recap experience make Once the most memory-led of
the three. Choose it when the emotional post-event presentation matters as much
as the camera used during the wedding.

## POV: best for customisation and sharing hardware

POV uses App Clips and Instant Apps so guests can enter without downloading the
full application. Hosts choose a style, cover photo, shot count and reveal
timing, while guests may also upload from their camera rolls.

POV extends beyond software with ready-to-print QR templates, the optional NFC
POV Block and a custom-designed photobook. Its business option can add branding,
a live slideshow and support.

## Lense: best for flexible collection and live display

Lense includes up to 25 captures per guest, optional filters, camera-roll
uploads, a shared album, one year of storage and a Live Photo Wall. Guests use
a QR code or link without an app or registration.

An unlimited-captures add-on removes the finite rule, while a separate video
add-on enables clips up to one minute. Lense is the easiest of the three to
choose when the venue display or mixed upload methods are requirements.

## Pricing structure

Each service offers a small free event and paid capacity based on guest count
or selected upgrades. Once starts free for five participants, POV for ten and
Lense for seven. Because the totals change with event size and optional
features, compare a realistic guest count rather than only the free tier.

## Which should you choose?

- Choose **Once** for custom development, styled film and voice/recap memories.
- Choose **POV** for App Clip entry, deeper event design, NFC and photobooks.
- Choose **Lense** for camera-roll uploads, a live wall, filters and video.

For a fourth approach with fixed roll options and fewer extras, see how a
