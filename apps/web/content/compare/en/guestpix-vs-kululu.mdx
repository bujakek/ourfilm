**The short answer:** GuestPix is the stronger choice for a long-lived,
organised wedding gallery with guestbooks and multiple albums. Kululu is the
stronger choice when a live photo wall should become part of the reception.

Both services collect guest photos and videos through a QR code or link without
requiring an app. Their priorities differ: GuestPix invests more in the album
around the event, while Kululu focuses on immediate participation during it.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, so it is not an independent review.
Features and plan details were checked against the official [GuestPix wedding
pricing page](https://guestpix.com/weddings-pricing/) and [Kululu pricing
page](https://www.kululu.com/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Prices, promotions and
inclusions may change.

## GuestPix vs Kululu at a glance

| Feature           | GuestPix                                     | Kululu                            |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Guest access      | QR code or link; no app or sign-up           | QR code or link; no app required  |
| Photos and videos | Yes                                          | Yes                               |
| Venue display     | Photo and video slideshow                    | Live Photo Wall                   |
| Guest messages    | Written and video guestbook                  | Captions on photo and video posts |
| Album structure   | Multiple albums on eligible plans            | One simpler digital event album   |
| Free use          | 50 photo uploads                             | 50 photo or video uploads         |
| Paid upload limit | Unlimited media; fair-use terms apply        | 500 on Plus; unlimited on Pro     |
| Retention         | Depends on package, up to multi-year windows | 3 months on Plus; 1 year on Pro   |

## Choose GuestPix for the post-wedding gallery

GuestPix treats the gallery as a wedding destination. Paid plans include photo
and video uploads, moderation, a live slideshow, written and video guestbooks,
likes, comments, printable templates and an invitation with RSVP.

Its higher packages add custom visual themes, more album organisation and
longer contribution windows. That is useful when guests or photographers will
continue adding media after the wedding and the collection needs structure.

## Choose Kululu for participation at the venue

Kululu's Live Photo Wall is included across its plans. New photos and videos
can appear on a television, projector or venue screen as guests contribute
them. Captions add context and make the wall feel more like a shared event feed.

The paid plans are easier to compare by total upload volume and retention:
Plus allows up to 500 uploads, while Pro offers unlimited uploads subject to
its terms and includes moderation. Kululu is therefore easier to frame around
the expected activity at one event.

## Free plans and pricing structure

Both free offers stop at 50 contributions, but the limits are not identical.
GuestPix's free gallery accepts 50 photos and stays open for a 30-day upload
window. Kululu accepts 50 photos or videos, stays active for 24 hours and keeps
uploads for seven days.

Both sell one-time paid options. GuestPix packages vary by gallery features and
upload/hosting windows. Kululu's Plus and Pro plans vary by upload allowance,
active period, retention and moderation. Kululu regularly displays promotional
pricing, so compare the live checkout rather than an older quoted amount.

## The deciding question

Choose GuestPix if the organised gallery, guestbook and post-event contribution
window matter most. Choose Kululu if the venue screen and real-time social
energy are the centre of the experience.

If neither breadth is necessary and you want a finite, no-preview guest camera,
[compare the digital disposable-camera approach](/en/digital-disposable-camera-for-weddings)
as a third option.
