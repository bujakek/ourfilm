**The short answer:** choose GuestPix when the priority is a polished,
interactive media gallery with multiple album options. Choose Wedibox when the
same QR code should also support a wedding website, audio guestbook, RSVP and
seating tools.

Both products collect guest photos and videos in the browser without an app.
GuestPix goes deeper into gallery presentation and social interaction; Wedibox
goes wider into the rest of the guest journey.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review.
Product and plan details were checked against the official [GuestPix wedding
pricing page](https://guestpix.com/weddings-pricing/) and [Wedibox pricing
page](https://www.wedibox.com/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Prices and inclusions may
change.

## GuestPix vs Wedibox at a glance

| Feature         | GuestPix                                   | Wedibox                                 |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Guest access    | QR code or private link; no app or sign-up | QR code or link; no app or login        |
| Media           | Photos, videos and guestbook messages      | Photos, videos and voice messages       |
| Live display    | Photo and video slideshow                  | Live slideshow                          |
| Guestbook       | Written and video                          | Digital and audio                       |
| Albums          | Multiple albums on eligible plans          | Unlimited albums on paid wedding plans  |
| Wedding website | Digital invitation and RSVP                | Wedding website with custom Wedibox URL |
| Planning tools  | RSVP included with wedding packages        | RSVP, meals and seating on All-In-One   |
| Free use        | 50 photo uploads                           | 50 photo uploads                        |
| Paid model      | One-time wedding packages                  | $29 Embed, $49 Wedding, $79 All-In-One  |

## Where GuestPix is stronger

GuestPix is designed as a premium shared gallery. Paid plans include unlimited
photos and videos subject to fair-use terms, moderation, likes and comments,
written and video guestbooks, a live slideshow and more than 180 editable Canva
templates.

Signature plans add custom themes, fonts and multiple album organisation. The
long upload windows are useful when photographers or guests will continue
contributing well after the wedding day.

## Where Wedibox is stronger

Wedibox makes media one part of a larger wedding portal. Its Wedding plan adds
a wedding website, audio guestbook, live slideshow, unlimited guest uploads and
original-quality ZIP download. The All-In-One plan adds detailed RSVPs, meals,
plus-ones, a live seating chart, music and registry links.

Wedibox also offers a separate Embed plan for couples who already have a
wedding website and only need a gallery widget.

## Storage and upload windows

GuestPix plans currently offer different upload and hosting periods: the
Classic package lists three months of uploads and 12 months of hosting, while
higher packages extend the contribution window.

Wedibox lists a three-month upload window for Embed, six months for Wedding and
one year for All-In-One. Storage runs for at least a year, and two years on the
top plan. Those dates matter more than the headline price if guests will upload
late.

## The deciding question

Choose GuestPix when visual gallery design, interaction and guestbook formats
matter most. Choose Wedibox when consolidating the wedding website, RSVPs,
seating and media behind one QR code will replace several other tools.

If you only need guests to take a limited set of new photos, a
[camera-first wedding experience](/en/digital-disposable-camera-for-weddings)
may be simpler than either platform.
