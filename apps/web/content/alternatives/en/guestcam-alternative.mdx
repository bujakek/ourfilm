OurFilm is a GuestCam alternative for couples who want a focused digital
disposable camera rather than an unlimited wedding media gallery. Guests scan
one QR code, enter a name and take a finite number of photos in their browser.

**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a no-preview, limited-roll wedding
camera with a controlled reveal. Choose GuestCam when unlimited photo and video
uploads, an audio guestbook, reactions, comments and a live slideshow belong in
the same platform.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, so it is not an independent review.
GuestCam features and pricing were checked against its official [wedding
page](https://guestcam.co/photo-sharing/weddings) and [pricing
page](https://guestcam.co/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Prices and inclusions may
change.

## OurFilm versus GuestCam at a glance

| Feature              | OurFilm                               | GuestCam                                      |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Guest access         | QR code or link in the mobile browser | QR code or link, with no app or guest account |
| Core format          | New photos on a finite personal roll  | Unlimited photo and video uploads             |
| Roll or upload limit | 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 photos per guest  | Unlimited guests, photos and videos           |
| Reveal               | Immediately or at event end           | Live gallery and slideshow                    |
| Camera-roll uploads  | No                                    | Yes                                           |
| Video                | No                                    | Yes                                           |
| Audio guestbook      | No                                    | Included; phone option on higher plans        |
| Public pricing       | Free for 5 guests; one event upgrade  | Standard $49; Premium $97                     |

## What GuestCam adds

GuestCam is a broader collection platform. Guests can contribute photos and
videos, record voice messages, react and comment, and watch uploads appear in a
live slideshow. Its listed plans include unlimited guests and media, with the
upload window, storage period, number of main galleries and host controls
changing by tier.

It also offers optional services such as face-based photo finding and a
dedicated audio guestbook phone number. GuestCam is therefore the stronger fit
when the wedding needs several media formats or a live social gallery.

## What OurFilm keeps deliberate

OurFilm gives every participant a host-selected roll of 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36
photos. There is no preview and no retake. The gallery can open immediately,
wait until the event ends or remain hidden from guests.

It does not accept existing phone photos, video or voice messages. That smaller
feature set protects one specific format: guests photograph the wedding in the
moment and discover the combined film later.

## Pricing model differences

GuestCam currently lists Standard at $49 and Premium at $97 per event. Both
include unlimited guests, photos and videos; Premium extends upload and storage
time and adds more galleries, themes and advanced collaborator controls.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. A one-time event purchase removes
the participant cap, while every guest keeps the finite roll selected by the
host.

## When GuestCam is the better choice

Choose GuestCam if you need:

- unlimited photo and video uploads;
- an audio guestbook;
- a live venue slideshow;
- reactions, comments or multiple galleries;
- long post-event upload and storage windows;
- advanced collaborator access or optional face search.

## When OurFilm is the better choice

Choose OurFilm if you want:

- a stricter digital disposable-camera format;
- no preview, no retake and a finite personal roll;
- only new photos captured during the event;
- an immediate or end-of-event reveal;
- one private wedding film without social extras;
- a lower-friction free trial for a very small event.

## Switching from GuestCam

OurFilm cannot import a GuestCam gallery. Download the existing media before
switching, create a new OurFilm event and replace every printed or digital QR
code. Test the new code on a guest phone before the wedding.
