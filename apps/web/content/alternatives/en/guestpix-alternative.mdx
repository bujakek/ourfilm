OurFilm is a GuestPix alternative for weddings that want guests to take a
limited set of new photos rather than upload unlimited media to a feature-rich
gallery.

**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a finite, no-preview guest camera and a
controlled reveal. Choose GuestPix for camera-roll uploads, video, multiple
albums, moderation, a live slideshow, guestbooks and RSVP tools.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, so it is not an independent review.
GuestPix features and plan details were checked against its official [wedding
pricing page](https://guestpix.com/weddings-pricing/) and [help
centre](https://help.guestpix.com/article/189-what-is-inlcuded-in-a-free-gallery)
on 2026-09-17. Prices, limits and promotions may change.

## OurFilm versus GuestPix at a glance

| Feature             | OurFilm                               | GuestPix                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Guest access        | QR code or link in the mobile browser | QR code or private link, with no app download |
| Core format         | New photos on a finite personal roll  | Shared photo and video upload gallery         |
| Free use            | Up to 5 participants                  | Up to 50 photo uploads                        |
| Paid media limit    | Finite roll per guest                 | Unlimited photos and videos, fair use applies |
| Reveal              | Immediately or at event end           | Live gallery and slideshow                    |
| Camera-roll uploads | No                                    | Yes                                           |
| Guestbook           | No                                    | Written and video guestbook                   |
| Albums and RSVP     | One event film; no RSVP tool          | Albums, digital invitation and RSVP           |

## What GuestPix adds

GuestPix is designed to gather a wide range of existing wedding media. Paid
plans include photo and video uploads, content moderation, a live slideshow,
written and video guestbook entries, printable QR templates and longer upload
windows. Higher plans add more albums and visual customisation.

Its free gallery provides a useful low-risk trial with up to 50 photo uploads.
That makes GuestPix the broader choice when collection volume and media variety
matter more than a disposable-camera rule.

## What OurFilm keeps simple

OurFilm opens a camera rather than an upload picker. Each guest receives 5, 10,
16, 24 or 36 frames and cannot preview or retake a committed shot. The host
decides when the gallery appears and whether guests may browse it.

There are no videos, guestbook messages, themes, comments, reactions or RSVP
features. The entire product is organised around one wedding film and the
experience of making it together.

## Pricing model differences

GuestPix sells several one-time wedding packages whose exact price depends on
the selected currency and plan. Its public offer currently includes a free
50-photo gallery, while paid tiers unlock unlimited photos and videos subject
to fair use, longer upload windows and additional gallery features.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. Its one-time event upgrade removes
the participant cap but preserves the chosen number of frames per person.

## When GuestPix is the better choice

Choose GuestPix if you need:

- existing camera-roll photos and videos;
- unlimited paid uploads rather than finite rolls;
- written or video guestbook messages;
- a live slideshow, reactions and comments;
- multiple albums, themes or co-hosts;
- an invitation and RSVP flow in the same service.

## When OurFilm is the better choice

Choose OurFilm if you want:

- a capture-first digital disposable camera;
- no preview and no retake;
- a specific roll length for every participant;
- a delayed first look at the wedding film;
- a private, focused photo experience;
- no gallery or planning features beyond what the camera needs.

## Switching from GuestPix

OurFilm cannot import a GuestPix gallery. Download the original media before
switching, create a new event and replace the QR code anywhere it has already
been printed or shared.
