OurFilm is a WedUploader alternative for couples who want guests to photograph
through a finite event camera instead of sending existing photos and videos
directly to Google Drive.

**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a no-preview personal roll and a
controlled gallery reveal. Choose WedUploader when unlimited, original-quality
files should live in your own Google Drive and remain available without an
album expiry date.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, so it is not an independent review.
WedUploader features were checked against its official [product
page](https://weduploader.com/) and [pricing
page](https://weduploader.com/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Its pricing page loads the
current amount dynamically, so verify the live checkout before deciding.

## OurFilm versus WedUploader at a glance

| Feature             | OurFilm                                 | WedUploader                                  |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Guest access        | QR code or link in the mobile browser   | QR code or link, with no guest login         |
| Core format         | New photos on a finite personal roll    | Files uploaded directly to your Google Drive |
| Media limit         | 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 photos per guest    | Unlimited uploads, subject to Drive storage  |
| Reveal              | Immediately or at event end             | Real-time shared gallery; no film reveal     |
| Camera-roll uploads | No                                      | Yes                                          |
| Video               | No                                      | Yes                                          |
| Storage             | Private OurFilm event storage           | The host's connected Google Drive            |
| Expiry              | Event remains until the host deletes it | Albums are advertised as non-expiring        |

## What WedUploader adds

WedUploader sends guest photos and videos directly to a Google Drive connected
by the host. It advertises unlimited guests, uploads and albums, with the actual
capacity governed by the Drive account's available storage.

Its premium feature set includes a real-time gallery, captions and messages,
voice messages, custom layouts and colours, personalised links, event managers,
a fullscreen slideshow and controls for keeping selected media private.

This makes WedUploader the stronger option when ownership of the storage
location, original file quality and long-term access matter most.

## What OurFilm keeps different

OurFilm does not connect to Google Drive and does not expose an upload picker.
Guests take new still photos inside the event camera and work through a finite
host-selected roll. There is no preview or retake.

The host can reveal the gallery immediately, wait until the event ends or keep
guest viewing disabled. The finished album can then be downloaded together.

## Pricing model differences

WedUploader offers a one-time premium purchase that unlocks its wider feature
set across albums; the live amount is rendered dynamically on its pricing page.
Google includes a limited amount of Drive storage, and additional storage may
carry a separate Google One cost.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. One event purchase removes the
participant cap, while the finite roll remains part of the format.

## When WedUploader is the better choice

Choose WedUploader if you need:

- files delivered directly to your Google Drive;
- unlimited photo and video uploads;
- original uploaded files without an album download step;
- multiple non-expiring event albums;
- captions, voice messages or a live slideshow;
- control over the cloud storage account holding the files.

## When OurFilm is the better choice

Choose OurFilm if you want:

- a dedicated capture experience rather than a file uploader;
- a finite roll for every guest;
- no preview and no retake;
- an end-of-event reveal;
- no Google account connection during setup;
- one focused, private wedding film.

## Switching from WedUploader

The uploaded files already live in the host's Google Drive, but OurFilm cannot
import them into a new event. Create the OurFilm camera separately and replace
the old QR code before printing or sharing it.
