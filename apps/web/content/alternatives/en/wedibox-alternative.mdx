OurFilm is a Wedibox alternative for couples who need a focused guest camera,
not an all-in-one wedding website and planning platform.

**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a finite, no-preview photo roll with a
controlled reveal. Choose Wedibox when the same QR code should also handle
video, voice messages, a guestbook, RSVP, seating and a wedding website.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, so it is not an independent review.
Wedibox features and pricing were checked against its official [product
page](https://www.wedibox.com/) and [pricing
page](https://www.wedibox.com/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Prices and inclusions may
change.

## OurFilm versus Wedibox at a glance

| Feature          | OurFilm                               | Wedibox                                     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Guest access     | QR code or link in the mobile browser | QR code or link, with no app or login       |
| Core format      | New photos on a finite personal roll  | Wedding portal with media collection        |
| Free use         | Up to 5 participants                  | 50 guest photo uploads                      |
| Paid media limit | Finite roll per guest                 | Unlimited uploads on listed paid plans      |
| Reveal           | Immediately or at event end           | Live gallery and slideshow                  |
| Video and audio  | No                                    | Video uploads and audio guestbook           |
| Wedding planning | No RSVP, website or seating tools     | Website, RSVP and seating on relevant plans |
| Public pricing   | One event upgrade                     | Embed $29; Wedding $49; All-In-One $79      |

## What Wedibox adds

Wedibox combines wedding photo sharing with a wider guest portal. Its Wedding
plan includes unlimited photo and video uploads, a website, audio guestbook,
live slideshow and original-quality ZIP. The All-In-One plan adds RSVP, meal
choices, plus-ones, a seating chart, registry links and other website sections.

An Embed plan serves couples who already have a wedding website and only want
the media gallery inside it. Wedibox is therefore the stronger fit when one
system should cover planning information as well as guest media.

## What OurFilm keeps focused

OurFilm asks only for the settings needed by the camera: event end, roll
length, reveal timing and guest gallery access. Each participant takes 5, 10,
16, 24 or 36 new photos with no preview or retake.

It does not provide RSVP, seating, a wedding website, video or voice messages.
That narrower scope gives guests a direct path from QR code to shutter.

## Pricing model differences

Wedibox currently lists a $29 Embed plan, a $49 Wedding plan and a $79
All-In-One plan, each as a one-time purchase. Upload windows and included
planning tools vary by plan.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. Its one-time event purchase
removes the participant cap, while every participant keeps the selected finite
roll.

## When Wedibox is the better choice

Choose Wedibox if you need:

- photo and video uploads from guests' phones;
- an audio, written or video guestbook;
- a live slideshow;
- a wedding website or embedded gallery;
- RSVP, meal choices and plus-one management;
- a seating chart and guest seat lookup.

## When OurFilm is the better choice

Choose OurFilm if you want:

- only a digital disposable camera;
- a finite number of new photos per guest;
- no preview and no retake;
- an immediate or event-end reveal;
- one private wedding film;
- no planning portal between the QR code and the camera.

## Switching from Wedibox

OurFilm cannot import a Wedibox gallery, guest list or seating plan. Download
the media you need, create a new event and replace the QR code everywhere it
has already been placed.
