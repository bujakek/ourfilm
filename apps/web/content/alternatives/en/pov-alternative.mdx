OurFilm is a POV alternative for weddings that need a simple, photo-only
digital disposable camera. Guests scan a QR code, enter their name and take a
limited number of photos in their browser. There is no app download, guest
account or camera-roll upload.

**The short answer:** choose OurFilm when you want fewer settings and a direct
browser camera. Choose POV when App Clips or Instant Apps, styled cameras,
camera-roll uploads, printable templates, physical NFC products or photobooks
are important.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, not an independent reviewer. POV
features and pricing were checked on its [official site](https://pov.camera/),
[wedding page](https://pov.camera/use-cases/weddings) and [pricing
calculator](https://pov.camera/pricing) on 2026-08-29. Plans may change.

## OurFilm versus POV at a glance

| Feature                      | OurFilm                             | POV                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Guest access                 | QR code or link in a mobile browser | QR code or link through App Clips and Instant Apps   |
| Full app download for guests | Not required                        | Not required                                         |
| Shots per guest              | 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36                 | Customisable by the host                             |
| Reveal                       | Immediately or at event end         | Host-controlled reveal timing                        |
| Camera-roll uploads          | No                                  | Yes                                                  |

| Additional products          | None                                | Photobooks and broader event options                 |
| Free use                     | Up to 5 participants                | Public calculator offers up to 10 guests free        |

## Why couples choose POV

POV is a mature, customisable event product. It supports both live capture and
camera-roll uploads, offers designed QR materials and extends the experience
with products such as its NFC block and photobook.

That breadth makes POV a better choice if you want the photo tool to become a
larger part of the event design, or if guests must add pictures they took
outside the shared camera.

## Why couples choose OurFilm

OurFilm is narrower by design. It focuses on the act of taking a limited number
of new photos during the event. There is no upload library, filter picker,
video, slideshow or physical accessory.

The host controls:

- when the event camera closes;
- whether photos appear immediately or at the event end;
- whether guests can see the final gallery;
- whether each person gets 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 shots.

The event is private and unindexed, unwanted photos can be hidden and the full
ready gallery can be downloaded as a ZIP.

## Pricing model differences

POV's public calculator prices events by guest count and offers a free option
for up to ten guests. Optional business features can change the total.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. Its one-time event upgrade removes
the participant cap, while every guest still keeps the finite roll chosen by
the host. Guests do not pay in either product.

Check the live pricing page of either service before buying; event tiers and
regional prices can change after this comparison is published.

## When POV is the better choice

Choose POV when you need one or more of these:

- uploads from the phone's existing camera roll;
- more visual camera and event customisation;
- App Clip or Instant App guest entry;
- ready-made QR code designs or the physical POV Block;
- a photobook path after the event;
- a broader feature set for professional or branded events.

## When OurFilm is the better choice

Choose OurFilm when you want a deliberately small workflow:

- one private browser camera;
- one chosen roll size for every participant;
- no preview or retake;
- immediate or event-end development;
- one complete gallery download;
- no need for uploads, filters, video or live displays.

## Moving an event from POV

There is no automatic POV import. Download anything you want to keep, create a
new OurFilm event and test the final QR code before changing printed materials.
Existing POV links and QR codes will not redirect to OurFilm.
