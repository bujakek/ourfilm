OurFilm is a Once alternative for couples who want a focused, browser-based
digital disposable camera: guests scan a QR code, enter their name and receive
a finite roll of photos without downloading an app or creating an account.

**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a lean photo-only experience with
simple controls. Choose Once if visual film styles, custom development timing,
recap features or its more established international product matter to you.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, so it is not an independent review.
Once features and public pricing were checked against its [official wedding
page](https://once.film/wedding), [pricing page](https://once.film/pricing) and
App Store listing on 2026-08-29. Plans and regional prices may change.

## OurFilm versus Once at a glance

| Feature            | OurFilm                                       | Once                                                            |

| Guest access       | QR code or link in the mobile browser         | QR code or link; guests can join without a full app download    |

## Where OurFilm is different

OurFilm deliberately has a smaller surface area. It is built around one camera,
one finite roll per guest and one private gallery. The host chooses the event
end, shot count, reveal behaviour and whether guests can see the finished
gallery.

The current product does not include film filters, video, audio messages, recap
videos, live slideshows or camera-roll uploads. That makes it easier to explain,
but it also means Once is the stronger choice when those extras are part of the
experience you want.

## When Once is the better choice

Choose Once when you want:

- a more established international disposable-camera product;
- visual film styling and broader camera customisation;
- a reveal scheduled beyond the event end;
- recap or voice-guestbook features;
- participant tiers that match a specific event size.

Those are meaningful differences, not decorative checklist items. There is no
reason to choose a simpler product if you already know you will miss one of
them.

## When OurFilm is the better fit

Choose OurFilm when your requirements are narrower:

- guests must join in a browser without registration;
- every participant should receive the same 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36-shot roll;
- photos should reveal immediately or when the event ends;
- the event should stay private and unindexed;
- the couple needs a complete ZIP download;
- you do not need filters, video, audio or recap features.

The free event supports up to five participants. A one-time upgrade admits
unlimited participants, while the per-person roll remains finite.

## Switching from Once

OurFilm does not import an existing Once event or gallery. If you have already
used Once, download the files you want to keep before moving. Create and test a
new OurFilm event before printing replacement QR cards.

If the event has not started, compare the real guest flows on more than one
phone. The most important test is not the host dashboard; it is how quickly a
guest reaches the shutter from a printed QR code.
