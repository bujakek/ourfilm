**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a compact browser camera with a fixed
finite roll. Choose POV when App Clip or Instant App entry, camera-roll uploads,
deeper styling, printable assets, NFC products or photobooks matter.

Both products turn a QR code into a digital disposable camera, but POV extends
further into event design and physical products. OurFilm concentrates on the
capture rule and the private developed album.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review. POV
features and plan details were checked against its [official
site](https://pov.camera/), [wedding page](https://pov.camera/use-cases/weddings)
and [pricing calculator](https://pov.camera/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Plans may
change.

## Guest technology and access

OurFilm uses the mobile browser directly. A guest opens the event, enters a
name and reaches the camera without registration. This gives it a consistent,
plain guest journey across supported phones.

POV uses lightweight platform experiences such as Apple's App Clips and
Android Instant Apps so guests do not need the full application. That can feel
more app-like and is a meaningful distinction if those native entry points are
part of the experience you want.

## Camera rules and additional features

OurFilm's rules are fixed and visible: the host selects 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36
shots for every participant. There is no preview, retake, camera-roll upload,
filter, video or unlimited mode.

POV offers a wider customisation layer. Its product includes host-controlled
shot and reveal settings, existing-photo uploads, designed sharing materials
and optional physical products. It is better suited to an event where the
camera should fit a larger visual system.

## Pricing models

POV's public calculator prices personal events by guest count and offers a free
option for up to ten guests. Business essentials and other choices can affect
the total.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. Its one-time event purchase
removes the participant cap but leaves the finite per-person roll in place.
Check the live calculators before buying.

## Choose POV when

- guests should upload existing camera-roll photos;
- App Clip or Instant App entry is attractive;
- styled camera and QR assets are important;
- you want an NFC block or a photobook path;
- business and branded event options may be needed.

## Choose OurFilm when

- a direct browser camera is your preference;
- the same finite roll should apply to everyone;
- no preview or retake is a firm creative rule;
- the gallery should reveal immediately or at event end;
- you do not need uploads, physical products or extensive styling.
