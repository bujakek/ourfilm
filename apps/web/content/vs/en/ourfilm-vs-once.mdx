**The short answer:** both products recreate a disposable camera on guests'
phones. Choose OurFilm for a lean browser camera with fixed roll options and two
simple reveal states. Choose Once for more visual styling, flexible development
timing and additional memory features.

This is a close comparison at the concept level. Guests scan a QR code, take a
limited set of pictures and wait to see what the shared film contains. The
decision depends less on the basic camera and more on how much customisation
you want around it.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review. Once
features and pricing structure were checked against its official [wedding
page](https://once.film/wedding) and [pricing page](https://once.film/pricing)
on 2026-09-17. Plans and regional prices may change.

## OurFilm vs Once at a glance

| Feature            | OurFilm                             | Once                                          |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Guest access       | QR code or link in a mobile browser | QR code or link; no guest app download        |
| Guest account      | Not required                        | Not required for the advertised guest flow    |
| Shots per person   | 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36                 | Host-customised film rolls                    |
| Reveal             | Immediately or when the event ends  | Custom development timing                     |
| Preview and retake | No                                  | Disposable-camera experience                  |

| Paid structure     | One event upgrade                   | Participant-based tiers and optional upgrades |

## Where the experiences overlap

OurFilm and Once both use scarcity and anticipation instead of an ordinary
upload folder. The host controls the roll, guests contribute candid pictures
and the shared album can remain private.

Neither product is trying to replace the professional photographer. They give
the couple a second perspective: the table conversations, dance-floor details
and unplanned moments that guests see from inside the event.

## Where Once offers more

Once presents a more customisable international product. Its public wedding
page highlights camera settings, private or shared films, configurable shot
counts and development timing. It also promotes visual styles and additional
memory formats beyond OurFilm's still-photo gallery.

That breadth is useful when the digital film should match a particular visual
theme or become a more produced post-event experience.

## Where OurFilm stays simpler

OurFilm asks the host to choose the event end, one of five roll lengths,
immediate or end-of-event development, and whether guests may view the album.
There is no filter picker, custom later date, video, audio message or recap
tool.

The guest experience is correspondingly direct: scan, enter a name and reach
the shutter. Every committed shot counts, with no preview or retake.

## Pricing models

Once prices an event according to the number of people who can join, with
optional upgrades. OurFilm is free for up to five participants, then offers a
one-time event purchase that removes the participant cap without removing the
finite roll. Check the live checkout for the final regional price.

## Choose Once when

- you want more visual camera and film styling;
- reveal timing must extend beyond the event end;
- recap or voice-guestbook features matter;
- a mature international product is a priority;
- participant-based tiers fit your event plan.

## Choose OurFilm when

- a browser-only, photo-only camera is enough;
- 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 shots cover the format you want;
- immediate or end-of-event development is sufficient;
- you want fewer host decisions and a focused guest flow;
- the final private gallery and ZIP are the desired output.
