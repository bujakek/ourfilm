**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a focused digital disposable camera.
Choose Wedibox when photo and video collection should sit inside a broader
wedding website with guestbook and planning tools.

Both services give guests QR access without requiring an app. Their scope is
very different: OurFilm makes taking limited, unseen pictures the activity;
Wedibox brings media collection and wedding coordination into one guest hub.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review.
Wedibox features and prices were checked against its official [product
page](https://www.wedibox.com/) and [pricing
page](https://www.wedibox.com/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Prices and package
inclusions may change.

## A camera format versus an all-in-one service

OurFilm exists to create one behaviour: guests take a small number of new
photos without seeing the result. The host controls the roll length, event end,
reveal and gallery visibility. It does not try to organise the wedding.

Wedibox combines media with a larger guest-facing site. Depending on the plan,
couples can collect photos, videos and audio messages, show a live slideshow,
publish wedding information, manage RSVPs and meal choices, and work with
seating or registry tools.

## What the guest experiences

An OurFilm guest enters a name and reaches the shutter. Existing camera-roll
files cannot be uploaded, committed photos cannot be previewed or retaken, and
the gallery can stay closed until the event is over.

A Wedibox guest can contribute media and interact with a broader wedding hub.
That is more capable, but it does not impose the same disposable-camera
constraint. Decide whether the constraint or the convenience is the product
you want.

## Pricing models

Wedibox currently lists one-time Embed, Wedding and All-In-One packages. The
Wedding tier covers the core wedding media experience, while higher packages
add more planning features. It also advertises a limited free trial.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. A one-time event purchase removes
the participant cap while preserving the finite roll for each guest.

## Choose Wedibox when

- photos, videos and audio messages should live together;
- you need a live slideshow;
- the same service should provide a wedding website;
- RSVP, meals, seating or registry tools are useful;
- unlimited media collection is more important than finite rolls.

## Choose OurFilm when

- taking pictures should feel like using a disposable camera;
- every guest needs the same limited number of shots;
- no preview and no retake are important;
- the gallery should stay hidden until the event ends;
- you already have separate wedding-planning tools.
