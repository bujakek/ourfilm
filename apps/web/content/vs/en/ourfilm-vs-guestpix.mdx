**The short answer:** choose OurFilm when you want guests to take a limited set
of new, unreviewed photos. Choose GuestPix when you want to collect a larger mix
of existing photos, videos, messages and event information in one gallery.

Both products let wedding guests join through a QR code without downloading an
app. The important difference is what happens next: OurFilm opens a finite
camera, while GuestPix opens a broader media-sharing experience.

This comparison is published by OurFilm, so it is not an independent review.
GuestPix features were checked against its official [wedding pricing
page](https://guestpix.com/weddings-pricing/) and [free-gallery help
page](https://help.guestpix.com/article/189-what-is-inlcuded-in-a-free-gallery)
on 2026-09-17. Plans, limits and regional prices may change.

## The biggest difference is capture versus collection

OurFilm treats the phone as a disposable camera. The host chooses one roll
length for everyone. Guests cannot preview or retake a committed photo, and
they cannot add polished pictures from their camera roll. The uncertainty is
part of the event format.

GuestPix is designed to collect more of what guests already have. Its paid
plans support photos and videos, content moderation, live slideshows, multiple
albums and guestbook contributions. That is more useful when completeness is
the goal.

## Reveal and guest participation

OurFilm can reveal the album immediately or hold every photo until the event
ends. The host can also keep the final gallery private from guests. This makes
the first look a deliberate moment.

GuestPix centres a live gallery and slideshow. Guests can contribute and see
the wider event collection as it develops. If the screen at the venue is part
of the entertainment, GuestPix has the stronger feature set.

## Pricing models

GuestPix offers a free gallery with up to 50 photo uploads and several paid
wedding packages. Paid plans expand the upload window and unlock unlimited
photos and videos subject to fair use, with inclusions varying by tier.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. A one-time event purchase removes
the participant cap, but each guest still receives the finite roll chosen by
the host. Check both live pricing pages before buying.

## Choose GuestPix when

- guests need to upload existing photos or videos;
- you want a written or video guestbook;
- a live slideshow, comments or reactions matter;
- you need multiple albums, themes, invitations or RSVP tools;
- collecting as much media as possible matters more than a shot limit.

## Choose OurFilm when

- you want a no-preview, no-retake camera game;
- every guest should receive the same finite roll;
- photos should stay hidden until the event ends;
- you want a private, photo-only gallery and full download;
- a smaller guest flow is more valuable than additional features.
