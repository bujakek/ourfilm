**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a private digital disposable camera
with a finite roll and an optional end-of-event reveal. Choose Kululu when a
live photo wall, video uploads and an active social gallery are central to the
party.

Both services use a QR code and work without a guest app. They create different
behaviour: OurFilm makes every shutter press scarce, while Kululu encourages an
ongoing stream of media that can appear on a venue screen.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review.
Kululu details were checked against its official [pricing
page](https://www.kululu.com/pricing) and [product
description](https://help.kululu.com/en/articles/11402245-what-is-kululu) on
2026-09-17. Prices, promotions and plan limits may change.

## Finite film versus a live event feed

OurFilm is built around restraint. Each participant gets the same host-selected
number of frames and cannot preview or retake a committed shot. The camera can
close with the event, and the complete film can remain hidden until then.

Kululu is designed for participation in public. Photos and videos can appear
on its Live Photo Wall as guests contribute them. That makes the collection
itself part of the entertainment and works well when a projector or television
is already planned.

## What each product leaves out

OurFilm does not provide video, captions, a live slideshow or an unlimited-shot
mode. It also does not accept files from the phone library. It keeps the guest
journey focused on taking a new photo.

Kululu does not use a fixed per-person film roll as its defining rule. Its free
and paid plans instead control the total upload allowance, active period and
storage period. If scarcity is the creative mechanism you want, those are not
equivalent experiences.

## Pricing models

Kululu has a free tier and paid Plus and Pro plans. The official page lists
different upload allowances, active periods and retention periods, and it may
show temporary promotional prices. Check the live total rather than relying on
an older comparison.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. Its one-time event purchase
removes the participant cap but does not remove the finite roll.

## Choose Kululu when

- a live venue photo wall is part of the programme;
- guests should contribute videos as well as photos;
- captions and an active shared feed are useful;
- you prefer a total upload allowance to a per-person shot limit;
- the album should entertain people during the event.

## Choose OurFilm when

- every guest should receive a specific roll length;
- no preview and no retake are important rules;
- the first look should wait until the event ends;
- you want a quieter, private photo-only experience;
- a complete ZIP is enough after the event.
