**The short answer:** choose OurFilm for a strict, photo-only disposable camera
with no preview or retake. Choose Lense when you also want camera-roll uploads,
filters, optional video, unlimited-capture upgrades or a live photo wall.

Both products give each guest a browser camera from a QR code. Lense surrounds
that camera with more ways to contribute and display media; OurFilm keeps the
finite roll as the whole experience.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review.
Lense features and prices were checked against its official [wedding
page](https://lense.app/disposable-camera-app-for-weddings) and [pricing
page](https://lense.app/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Prices and inclusions may change.

## OurFilm vs Lense at a glance

| Feature             | OurFilm                             | Lense                                      |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Guest access        | QR code or link; no account or app  | QR code or link; no guest app or sign-up   |
| Standard limit      | 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 shots per guest | Up to 25 captures per guest                |
| Unlimited capture   | No                                  | Optional one-time add-on                   |
| Reveal              | Immediately or when the event ends  | Instant, delayed or private reveal         |
| Camera-roll uploads | No                                  | Yes                                        |
| Filters             | No filter currently offered         | Optional filter effects                    |
| Video               | No                                  | Optional add-on for clips up to one minute |
| Live display        | No                                  | Live Photo Wall included                   |
| Free use            | Up to 5 participants                | Up to 7 guests                             |

## The products start similarly, then diverge

In either product a guest can scan, enter the event and begin taking pictures
without installing an app. Both can preserve the feeling of a limited camera.

Lense then adds flexibility. Guests can contribute pictures already on their
phones, hosts can select visual effects, every event includes a live slideshow,
and paid add-ons can enable videos or remove the standard 25-capture limit.

OurFilm does not offer those escape routes. The selected 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36
frames stay finite. A committed photo has no preview or retake, and only new
still photographs enter the event film.

## Reveal and privacy

OurFilm gives the host two development choices: reveal immediately or when the
event ends. The final gallery can be hidden from guests, remains unindexed and
is available to the host as a ZIP download.

Lense advertises instant, delayed and private reveal options, plus moderation
and a Live Photo Wall. It is the stronger choice when display and gallery
control need to extend beyond OurFilm's two simpler states.

## Pricing models

Lense prices events by guest count. Its public page lists a free plan for up to
seven guests and paid tiers through larger events. Unlimited captures and video
are separate one-time add-ons.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. One event purchase removes the
participant cap while preserving the selected roll for each guest.

## Choose Lense when

- guests need to add camera-roll photos;
- a live slideshow is required at the venue;
- filters or video clips matter;
- you want an option to remove the capture limit;
- guest-count tiers suit the size of your event.

## Choose OurFilm when

- the finite roll must remain a firm rule;
- no preview and no retake define the experience;
- only photos taken in the shared camera should appear;
- immediate or event-end development is enough;
- you prefer fewer controls and no add-ons.
