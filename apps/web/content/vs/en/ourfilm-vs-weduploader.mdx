**The short answer:** choose OurFilm when you want the act of taking limited,
unseen photos to be part of the wedding. Choose WedUploader when the practical
goal is to send guests' existing photos and videos directly into your Google
Drive.

Both products remove the need for a guest app and can begin with a QR code.
They solve different jobs: OurFilm is an event camera; WedUploader is a file
collection route with optional gallery features.

This comparison is published by OurFilm and is not an independent review.
WedUploader details were checked against its official [product
page](https://weduploader.com/) and [pricing
page](https://weduploader.com/pricing) on 2026-09-17. Its live price is rendered
dynamically, so confirm the current total before purchasing.

## Capture-first versus upload-first

OurFilm opens a camera. Guests cannot choose polished pictures from their
library, preview a committed frame or take it again. Each person works within
the same finite roll, and the resulting gallery can remain hidden until the
event ends.

WedUploader opens an upload route. Guests select the photos and videos already
on their phones, and the original files move into a folder in the couple's
Google Drive. That is efficient when the priority is ownership and collection,
not recreating a disposable camera.

## Storage and gallery ownership

WedUploader's direct-to-Drive model is its clearest advantage. Albums do not
depend on a separate expiring host library, and capacity is governed by the
couple's Google Drive storage. Premium adds display and presentation options
such as a live gallery, captions, slideshow and custom layouts.

OurFilm stores the private event film and gives the host a complete ZIP
download after development. A Google account is not required, but the service
is not a permanent Drive ingestion pipeline.

## Pricing models

WedUploader provides its core uploading service free and sells a Premium
upgrade for additional gallery, design and management features. Because the
live price is loaded dynamically, this comparison does not hard-code a number.

OurFilm is free for up to five participants. Its one-time event upgrade removes
the participant cap but keeps each participant's finite roll.

## Choose WedUploader when

- the files should arrive directly in your Google Drive;
- guests need to add existing photos and videos;
- original-quality file collection is the main requirement;
- an upload album should not expire with a gallery provider;
- optional live gallery and slideshow tools are useful.

## Choose OurFilm when

- guests should take new photographs inside one shared camera;
- a limited roll changes how people shoot;
- no preview and no retake are part of the fun;
- the album should reveal immediately or at event end;
- a private event gallery and ZIP meet your needs.
