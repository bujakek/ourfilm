Wedding photo uploads need an internet connection, but not necessarily venue
Wi-Fi. Reliable mobile data can be enough. Provide guest Wi-Fi when signal is
weak, many visitors are roaming or the venue blocks coverage.

## Ask the venue specific questions

Do not ask only whether Wi-Fi exists. Ask:

- how many guests the network supports;
- whether there is a separate guest network;
- where signal is weakest;
- whether upload traffic is limited;
- how the password will be shared;
- who can restart or troubleshoot it during the event.

## Test under realistic conditions

Visit the ceremony, dining room, bar and outdoor areas with an iPhone and
Android phone. Upload a real photo through the final service. A speed test near
the router does not represent a crowded venue.

## Make the password usable

Use a short readable password and display it separately from the photo QR code.
Connecting to Wi-Fi and joining the camera are two different actions; combining
both codes without labels creates confusion.

## Plan for interruption

Choose a guest-photo tool that clearly reports pending uploads and can retry
after connectivity returns. If the tool cannot queue work, tell guests to keep
their original files until confirmation appears.

For destinations with international guests, Wi-Fi also avoids roaming costs.

flow](/en/qr-code-for-wedding-photos) at the venue before printing final
instructions.
