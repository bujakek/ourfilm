**In short:** place the wedding photo QR code where guests have time, light and
a reason to use it. Table cards, the bar, guestbook and photo corner usually
outperform one large sign at the entrance.

## Dinner tables

Tables give guests time to read, scan and understand the activity. Use one card
per table or incorporate the code into a menu-sized insert. Keep it visible
after plates and centrepieces arrive.

## Bar and refreshment area

People pause at the bar and often have a phone in hand. Place the sign away from
spills and reflective surfaces, with enough light for scanning.

## Guestbook and gift table

This area already asks guests to contribute something. Pairing the camera with
the guestbook connects written and visual memories, but avoid crowding a narrow
queue.

## Photo corner

Put the code beside the lighting or props so guests can enter the shared camera
before posing. If the tool only accepts new captures, say so explicitly.

## Dance-floor edge

A small late-evening sign can revive participation after dinner. Keep it far
enough from movement and drinks that people can focus the camera safely.

## Entrance signs are reminders, not the whole plan

Arrival is busy. Guests are greeting people, checking seating and putting away
coats. Use the entrance for awareness, then repeat the same code at the moment
of use.

Pair placement with a clear [wedding photo QR-code
message](/en/qr-code-for-wedding-photos) and one spoken announcement.
