Ask guests to participate while they are already at the wedding and taking
photos. Introduce the camera or album before dinner, repeat the QR code where
people pause and send a next-day reminder only as a backup.

## Before the wedding

Include the photo experience in the wedding website or information email only
if guests can already join. Avoid creating a second temporary link that will
later be replaced.

Test the final event and print the exact same destination everywhere.

## On arrival

An entrance sign creates awareness, but arrival is too busy for most guests to
act. Repeat the code at tables and other calm locations.

## Before dinner or speeches

This is often the best spoken moment. The room is attentive, phones are nearby
and the reception still has many hours left. Keep the announcement under twenty
seconds.

## During the party

Use passive reminders at the bar, photo corner and dance-floor edge. Avoid
multiple announcements that interrupt the event or make participation feel
compulsory.

## After the event

If the tool accepts camera-roll uploads, send a thank-you message the next day.
If it is a capture-only disposable camera, a late upload request needs a
different destination because old files cannot be added.

For delayed galleries, reveal at the promised time and tell guests how long the
album remains available. Plan the complete [wedding guest photo
flow](/en/wedding-photo-sharing-app) before deciding the reminders.
