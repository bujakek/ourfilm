To share wedding photos in original quality, use a service that accepts and
exports the original file, then verify a downloaded sample. “HD,” “high
quality” and “full resolution” are product labels; the file's dimensions,
format and size provide the real answer.

## Where quality is commonly lost

Messaging and social platforms often optimise images for fast viewing. Editing
apps may resize exports, strip metadata or convert formats. Screenshots lose
more information and should never become the archive copy.

Cloud storage usually treats the upload as a file, while gallery services vary.
Read the export policy and test before inviting guests.

## A simple verification process

1. Take a known photo and note its pixel dimensions and file size.
2. Upload it through the exact guest flow.
3. Download it through the host export.
4. Compare dimensions, file type and visible detail.
5. Make a small print if large prints are important.

Metadata may legitimately be removed for privacy, especially location data.
That is separate from visible image resolution.

## Preserve two versions when useful

Keep originals or print-ready masters in a protected archive. Create smaller
copies for websites, chat and social posts. Do not repeatedly edit and resave
the same compressed copy.

When comparing a [wedding guest upload
tool](/en/wedding-guest-photo-upload), ask about maximum dimensions, video
limits, ZIP exports, retention and whether the host receives every original.
