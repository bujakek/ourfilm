**In short:** the safest wedding photo backup follows the 3-2-1 rule: keep at
least three copies, on two different types of storage, with one copy in another
location. An online gallery is convenient delivery, not a complete backup
strategy.

## Start with a verified download

Download professional files, guest uploads, videos and audio memories before
their service windows expire. Compare file counts, open several large files and
check that ZIP archives extract without errors.

Do not delete source galleries until the local copy and a second copy have both
been verified.

## Build a clear master folder

Use folders for originals, guest media, edited exports, video, audio and album
projects. Keep filenames stable and avoid editing originals in place. A simple
text file can record the photographer, download date, licence and any missing
items.

## Create independent copies

One practical arrangement is:

- working copy on a computer;
- second copy on an external drive stored safely;
- encrypted cloud copy in a separate account or service.

Two folders on the same laptop are one failure domain, not two backups.

## Test recovery

Once or twice a year, open random files from every copy and confirm the cloud
account, password recovery and external drive still work. Replace failing or
obsolete media before it becomes the only surviving copy.

Download the final [shared wedding photo
album](/en/shared-wedding-photo-album) early enough that a failed export can be
resolved while support and source files still exist.
