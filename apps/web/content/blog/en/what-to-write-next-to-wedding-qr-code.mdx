**In short:** the best text beside a wedding photo QR code tells guests what to
do and why it matters. Keep the main line emotional, then add the practical
detail beneath it.

## Short captions

1. **Scan. Shoot. Surprise us.**
2. **Help us capture what we cannot see.**
3. **Your view belongs in our wedding film.**
4. **One code. One camera. Everyone's memories.**
5. **Point, shoot and leave the reveal for later.**

## Elegant captions

6. **Photograph the moments between the moments.**
7. **Add your perspective to our shared wedding film.**
8. **A day seen through the eyes of everyone we love.**
9. **Scan to help us remember the whole celebration.**
10. **The official photographs tell one story. Help us tell the rest.**

## Playful captions

11. **You have 24 frames. Make them count.**
12. **No preview. No retake. Excellent decisions encouraged.**
13. **Caught something we missed? Put it on the roll.**
14. **Dance-floor evidence starts here.**
15. **Take the photo now. Judge it tomorrow.**

## Add the practical line

Under the headline, write something like:

> Scan the code, enter your name and open our shared camera. You have 24 photos.
> The album reveals when the party ends. No download or account needed.

Only include claims the selected service actually supports. If the gallery is
immediate, replace the final sentence. If guests upload existing files, say
“choose your photos” rather than “open the camera.”

Link the sign to the final [wedding photo QR
page](/en/qr-code-for-wedding-photos) and test the physical print before the
event.
