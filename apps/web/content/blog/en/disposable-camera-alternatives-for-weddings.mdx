The best alternative to disposable cameras is not always another camera. A
digital disposable camera preserves finite shots and anticipation; Instax gives
physical prints; a photo booth creates a set activity; and a QR upload album
collects the widest range of guest media.

## Digital disposable camera

Guests scan a QR code and receive a limited roll on their phones. There is no
film stock, collection or lab processing. Choose this when the creative rule
and delayed reveal matter more than physical negatives.

## Instax or instant cameras

Instant prints work beautifully in a guestbook and become a physical object
within minutes. Budget for film, batteries, supervision and misfires. Keep one
camera per active area to avoid queues.

## QR-code upload album

Guests add existing photos and videos from their own camera apps. This is the
best choice for completeness and often the weakest for creating a shared
activity. The request can feel like administration unless introduced well.

## Photo booth or self-service photo corner

A staffed booth provides reliable lighting, props and repeatable results. A
DIY corner can achieve a similar gathering point with a background, light and
phone stand at lower cost.

## Photographer table cameras

Assigning a camera to each table can create a genuine analogue look, but it
also creates uneven participation, lost equipment and development costs. Use
it only when authentic film is part of the design.

## Combine methods carefully

One main guest instruction is better than three competing QR codes. A sensible
combination is a finite [digital wedding guest
camera](/en/digital-disposable-camera-for-weddings) during the event and a
separate upload request for late videos or originals afterwards.
