Graduation party photo sharing works best when every guest receives one clear
destination before the celebration gets busy. A QR code can open a shared
upload album or a browser-based guest camera, but those experiences are not
interchangeable: an upload album collects pictures already taken on guests'
phones, while a guest camera invites them to capture new moments during the
event.

Choose the experience first, then generate the QR code. Check the school's and
venue's photography rules, test the complete guest journey and explain who can
see the finished gallery. The result should complement the official
photographer, not interrupt the ceremony.

## Pick the kind of graduation album you actually need

Start with the behaviour you want from guests rather than the QR code itself.

### A shared upload album collects existing pictures

Use an upload album when relatives and classmates will take photos in their
usual camera apps and contribute their favourites later. It is a practical
choice for polished group shots, pictures from several graduation-week events
or images guests may not upload until they get home.

Before choosing a service, check whether contributors need an account or app,
whether they can remove an accidental upload and when submissions close. An
unlimited upload promise can sound useful, but it may also leave the graduate
with hundreds of near-identical images to sort.

### A guest camera turns taking photos into an activity

A digital guest camera opens a camera after the scan and asks people to make
new pictures from where they stand. This works when you want the graduation to
feel like a shared disposable-camera roll: relatives notice reactions,
classmates capture one another and no single person has to document the whole
day.

OurFilm uses this model. Guests open the link in a browser, enter a name and
receive a finite roll chosen by the host: 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 frames. There is
no preview or retake. The host chooses whether the private gallery reveals
immediately, at the end or at a later time.

If the host enables the optional after-event window, a guest can add saved
photos for 24 hours after the event ends, using frames left on the original
roll. It is not an unlimited camera-roll uploader and it does not accept video.

### A photographer covers the moments that cannot be missed

A professional photographer is still the stronger choice for a formal class
portrait, stage walk or carefully lit family session. Guest photos solve a
different problem: they show the graduate's day from many seats and
relationships. If you use both, tell guests when phones must stay down and
where candid photography is welcome.

## Use one album or split the ceremony and party?

One album is simplest when the ceremony, open house and party happen on the
same day with mostly the same guests. The graduate receives one link, and the
story flows from gown and diploma details to food, speeches and the last group
photo.

Separate the collections when the school controls ceremony photography, the
party happens much later, or different groups need different access. Do not
print a private family-party link on a public school programme. If you make two
albums, name them by occasion instead of “Album 1” and “Album 2.”

For many families, the practical compromise is one guest album for the party
and the official school photographs kept separately. It reduces confusion and
avoids asking guests to scan anything during the formal ceremony.

## Check the rules before printing the QR code

Graduation venues often have tighter constraints than birthday parties. Ask
the school, college or venue:

- whether guest photography is allowed during the ceremony;
- whether flash, tripods or standing in aisles are prohibited;
- whether signs or cards may be placed in the venue;
- whether graduates, staff or children have photography restrictions;
- whether the ceremony is already being recorded or photographed officially.

Treat a “no photography” area as exactly that. A QR album does not override a
school policy, and a photo prompt should never encourage someone to block an
exit, cross a stage boundary or interrupt the procession.

If the formal venue cannot display the code, introduce it at the family lunch,
open house or reception instead. Guests can still capture the parts of the day
the official camera is least likely to see.

## Set up graduation photo sharing step by step

1. **Choose capture or upload.** Decide whether guests should make new photos,
   contribute existing ones or use a clearly limited combination.
2. **Create the final event.** Set its name, opening time, closing time, access
   and gallery reveal before generating anything for print.
3. **Test as a guest.** Scan on an iPhone and Android device, grant the needed
   permission and complete one real contribution without using the host login.
4. **Test the venue connection.** Try both guest Wi-Fi and mobile data where
   the reception, garden party or open house will happen.
5. **Print the final code with a short URL.** The URL gives guests a fallback
   if a damaged or reflective sign will not scan.
6. **Name an album steward.** One relative or friend should answer questions,
   make one announcement and review the collection afterwards.

Do this before invitations, table cards or welcome signs go to print. A QR code
can scan perfectly and still lead to the wrong draft event.

## Put the code where guests have time to use it

The entrance is useful for awareness, but it is rarely the best place to stop
and take a picture. Repeat the same code at a few natural pauses:

- beside the guest book or cards;
- on tables at the open house or family meal;
- near a photo wall, school-colour backdrop or memory display;
- by the dessert table after the formal moments;
- on a small card handed out by the host, if the venue permits it.

Avoid podiums, aisle edges and anywhere a sign could hide a reserved-seat or
accessibility notice. Keep the code large, high-contrast and surrounded by
white space. Test the actual printed size in the lighting guests will have.

## Give guests an instruction, not just a square

“Scan for photos” does not say whether guests should view, upload or take a
picture. Use one sentence that states the action, app requirement and reveal
time.

For an upload album:

> Add your graduation photos. Scan the code, choose your favourites and send
> them to our private shared album. Contributions close Sunday evening.

For a guest camera:

> Photograph graduation from your point of view. Scan, enter your name and use
> your 16 frames during the party. No app or account. We will reveal the album
> tomorrow.

Add a human cue as well. A host can mention the album after the welcome or ask
each table to contribute one photo before dessert. One timely reminder works
better than covering every surface in QR codes.

## Use prompts that tell the whole graduation story

The obvious diploma portrait matters, but the most personal collection usually
comes from what happens around it. Offer a short optional list:

- the graduate before everyone arrives;
- a parent or grandparent's reaction;
- cap, gown, cords, flowers or programme details;
- classmates together away from the official camera;
- the view from your seat;
- the person who helped behind the scenes;
- a before-and-after recreation from the first day of school;
- the first toast, cake cut or dance at the party.

Only suggest a cap toss where the school allows it and there is enough space.
Do not turn teachers, staff or other graduates into compulsory photo subjects.
Prompts should help people notice moments, not create a second schedule.

## Keep a graduation gallery appropriately private

A QR code is a visual link, not a password. Anyone who receives or photographs
it may be able to pass it on. Keep a private event code inside the invited
group, review the service's access settings and say who will be able to view or
download the gallery.

Graduation pictures may include children, name badges, certificates, school
logos and location details. Frame shots so personal information is not
needlessly readable. Give guests a named contact for hiding or removing a
picture, and review the album before sharing anything publicly.

Participation is not permission for social-media or promotional use. Ask
before posting someone else's portrait, especially when classmates or minors
appear. After the event, download the collection, keep a separate backup and
close contribution access when it is no longer needed.

## Graduation photo-sharing checklist

- [ ] The guest action is clearly capture, upload or a defined combination.
- [ ] School and venue photography rules have been checked.
- [ ] The final link works on iPhone, Android, Wi-Fi and mobile data.
- [ ] The sign explains app requirements and reveal timing.
- [ ] The QR code is away from aisles and formal ceremony areas.
- [ ] A host will make one announcement and answer questions.
- [ ] Guests know who can view the gallery.
- [ ] Review, download, backup and closure have an owner.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the easiest way to collect graduation party photos?

Use one tested shared destination and give everyone the same link or QR code.
Choose an upload album for pictures already on guests' phones or a guest camera
for new pictures taken during the celebration. Explain the action beside the
code and assign one person to manage the album.

### Do guests need an app for graduation photo sharing?

It depends on the service. OurFilm opens in a phone browser, so guests do not
need an app or account. Test the exact flow you choose before printing and do
not promise “no app” unless it is true for that service.

### Can I use a photo QR code during the graduation ceremony?

Only if the school or venue permits photography and signage. Formal ceremonies
may restrict phones, flash, standing or QR-card placement. When in doubt, keep
the guest album for the open house, family meal or party.

### Can guests upload existing graduation photos to OurFilm?

Only when the host enables the optional after-event window. For 24 hours after
the event ends, guests can use frames remaining on their original roll to add
saved photos. OurFilm does not accept video or offer unlimited uploads.

### Should the graduation gallery open during or after the party?

An immediate reveal suits a live social gallery. A later reveal keeps people
focused on the celebration and creates a film-development moment afterwards.
Choose the expectation before printing the sign and state it clearly.
