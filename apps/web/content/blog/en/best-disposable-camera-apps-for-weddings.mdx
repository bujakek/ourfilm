The best disposable camera app depends on which part of film you want to
recreate. OurFilm is the focused browser camera, Once emphasises styled film and
development, POV offers extensive customisation and physical products, and
Lense adds uploads, filters, video and a live photo wall.

This guide is published by OurFilm. Competitor features were checked on their
official product and pricing pages on 2026-09-17; plans can change.

## Quick comparison

| Product | Guest entry                   | Standard limit      | Strongest extra                         |
| ------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| OurFilm | Mobile browser, no account    | 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 | Strict no-preview, no-retake experience |
| Once    | QR or link, no full guest app | Custom film rolls   | Development controls and Voice Tapes    |
| POV     | App Clip or Instant App       | Host-customised     | Styling, NFC block and photobook        |
| Lense   | Browser, no guest sign-up     | Up to 25 per guest  | Live wall, uploads and optional video   |

## OurFilm

Choose OurFilm when the finite roll is the whole point. Guests take new photos
inside the browser camera, cannot preview or retake a committed frame and see
the gallery immediately or after the event ends. It does not support filters,
video or camera-roll uploads.

## Once

Choose Once for fully customisable rolls, flexible development timing, film
styling and additional memory formats. Its pricing follows participant count,
with a small free event and optional unlimited-shots upgrade.

## POV

Choose POV when design and event hardware matter. It combines App Clip or
Instant App entry with camera-roll uploads, styled QR materials, an NFC block
and a photobook option.

## Lense

Choose Lense when the camera must coexist with a broader gallery. It includes
camera-roll uploads, optional filters, a Live Photo Wall and paid upgrades for
unlimited capture or video clips.

## How to choose

Test the guest flow on real phones, then decide whether uploads, video, live
display and styling are requirements or distractions. Compare the complete
[digital disposable camera experience](/en/digital-disposable-camera-for-weddings),
not just free participant limits.
