A wedding photo guestbook combines a guest image with a message or story. Use
instant prints when you want a physical book during the reception, or collect
digital photos and messages when easier participation and better backup matter.

## Instax or Polaroid guestbook

Provide a camera, spare film, tested pens, photo-safe adhesive and one example
page. Leave space for prints to develop before sticking them down. Assign a
friend or attendant to check supplies.

## QR-code digital guestbook

Guests scan a code, upload a photo and write a message. This works across the
venue and can include people who cannot attend. Confirm whether the service
exports messages alongside the original media.

## Digital photos, printed later

Collect high-quality files during the wedding, then design the book after the
event. This gives better layout control and avoids blank pages, but the finished
object is not available immediately.

## Hybrid approach

Use instant prints for the physical book and a private QR album for backup and
additional pictures. Give the two methods different instructions so guests do
not wonder where to submit.

## Prompts for messages

Ask for a favourite memory, one piece of advice, a prediction for the next year
or a description of the wedding in three words. Optional prompts help without
making every page identical.

Link the digital part to one [wedding photo QR
code](/en/qr-code-for-wedding-photos), then download both images and message
data after the event.
