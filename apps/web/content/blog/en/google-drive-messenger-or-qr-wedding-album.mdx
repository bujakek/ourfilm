The best wedding photo-sharing method depends on the job. Google Drive is good
for controlled file storage, group chats are good for a small existing group,
shared albums are good for browsing, and a dedicated QR tool is good for giving
the entire guest list one consistent route.

## Quick comparison

| Method                | Best for                         | Main compromise                        |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Google Drive          | Original files and ownership     | Permissions and sign-in friction       |
| Messenger or WhatsApp | Small groups already in the chat | Noise, privacy and difficult archiving |
| Google/iCloud album   | Familiar visual browsing         | Account and device differences         |
| QR upload gallery     | Mixed wedding guest lists        | Another service to select and manage   |
| Digital guest camera  | Participation during the event   | Does not collect old camera-roll files |

## Guest effort matters most

Every extra account prompt, app switch and permission screen loses
contributors. Test the final route on a signed-out iPhone and Android phone.
Count the taps from QR scan to successful contribution, not merely to the first
page.

## Decide whether you need uploads or a camera

An upload-first tool asks guests to choose existing media. This is best when
the couple wants completeness, videos and files taken in other camera apps.

A camera-first tool asks guests to take new photos inside the event. This is
best when the collection method is part of the entertainment and the host wants
rules such as a finite roll or delayed reveal.

## Compare ownership and privacy

Ask where originals live, who can see the collection, whether guests can delete
other files, how long the album stays online and how the host downloads
everything. A familiar brand does not automatically mean the permissions match
your wedding.

For most mixed guest lists, start with a dedicated
[wedding photo-sharing app](/en/wedding-photo-sharing-app), then move to Drive
after downloading the final archive.
