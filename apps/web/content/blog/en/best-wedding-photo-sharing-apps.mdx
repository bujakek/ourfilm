The eight best wedding photo-sharing apps solve two different problems. OurFilm,
Once, POV and Lense create a digital disposable-camera experience. GuestPix,
Wedibox, Kululu and WedUploader focus more on collecting and presenting media
guests already captured.

This comparison is published by OurFilm. Product and pricing pages were checked
on 2026-09-17; regional prices and features may change.

## Eight options at a glance

| Product     | Primary experience         | Best fit                                    |
| ----------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| OurFilm     | Finite browser camera      | Strict no-preview, no-retake shared film    |
| Once        | Developed digital film     | Styling, flexible development and voice     |
| POV         | Custom event camera        | App Clip entry, design and photobook        |
| Lense       | Camera plus shared gallery | Uploads, filters, video and live wall       |
| GuestPix    | Rich media gallery         | Video, guestbooks and album organisation    |
| Wedibox     | Wedding guest portal       | Website, audio, slideshow, RSVP and seating |
| Kululu      | Live photo and video wall  | Real-time venue participation               |
| WedUploader | Google Drive collection    | Direct original-file ownership              |

## Best for a finite camera

Choose OurFilm for the smallest photo-only workflow, Once for a styled and
developed memory experience, POV for more design and physical products, or
Lense when uploads and venue display must sit beside the camera.

## Best for broad collection

Choose GuestPix for an organised interactive gallery, Wedibox for an all-in-one
wedding hub, Kululu for a live social wall or WedUploader when Google Drive
should be the final destination.

## What to compare

Test the real guest flow, not only the host dashboard. Check account and app
requirements, photo/video limits, moderation, reveal timing, upload window,
storage period, original-quality export and the final price for your guest
count.

Explore the underlying [digital disposable-camera
category](/en/digital-disposable-camera-for-weddings) before deciding between
capture-first and upload-first products.
