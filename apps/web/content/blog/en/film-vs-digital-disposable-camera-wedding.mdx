**In short:** choose real disposable cameras when the physical film process and
unpredictable analogue result are essential. Choose a digital disposable camera
when every guest should participate from their own phone and the couple needs
one downloadable gallery without collecting cameras or paying for development.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Question     | Film disposable camera               | Digital guest camera                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Equipment    | One physical camera per group        | Guests use their phones              |
| Shots        | Fixed by the roll                    | Host-selected digital limit          |
| Result       | Negatives, scans and possible prints | Private online album and download    |
| Delay        | Lab development                      | Software-controlled reveal           |
| Failure risk | Lost camera, flash or development    | Battery, browser or connection issue |
| Look         | Genuine film characteristics         | Digital image unless styled later    |

## Cost and logistics

Film requires cameras, development and scanning. Add spare units, transport
and the time required to collect every camera. Digital pricing usually follows
the event or guest count and avoids lab costs, but depends on phone access and
service retention.

## Image quality and character

Film has authentic grain, colour response, flash fall-off and mistakes. A
digital product should not claim to reproduce that merely by limiting shots.
Its advantage is convenience, easier backups and more predictable usable files.

## Guest behaviour

A physical camera becomes a shared object at the table. A digital camera gives
everyone access and can enforce an individual roll. Both create more deliberate
shooting than an unlimited upload folder.

Try the [QR-code digital disposable camera
flow](/en/digital-disposable-camera-for-weddings) before printing any signs,
and choose film only if its physical character justifies the extra logistics.
