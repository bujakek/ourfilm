**In short:** “Collect wedding photos” describes two different jobs.
Upload-first apps gather files guests already took; camera-first apps shape the
pictures while guests take them. The first usually produces more media, while
the second creates a stronger shared activity.

This guide is published by OurFilm. Public product information was reviewed on
2026-09-17; check live plans before purchasing.

## Best choices by goal

| Goal                             | Product to consider | Why                                       |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Strict browser guest camera      | OurFilm             | Fixed rolls, no preview and simple reveal |
| Styled developed film            | Once                | Custom rolls, development and voice       |
| App Clip and event customisation | POV                 | Fast entry, styling and physical products |
| Filters, uploads and live wall   | Lense               | Wider camera and gallery feature set      |
| Photos, video and guestbook      | GuestPix            | Rich organised wedding gallery            |
| Wedding website and planning     | Wedibox             | Media, audio, RSVP and seating            |
| Live venue photo wall            | Kululu              | Real-time social display                  |
| Direct Google Drive collection   | WedUploader         | Original files in host-owned storage      |

## Camera-first tools

OurFilm, Once, POV and Lense make the capture experience part of the wedding.
Compare shot limits, reveal timing, guest technology, filters, uploads, video
and live display. A finite roll is a creative rule, not a missing unlimited
plan.

## Upload-first tools

GuestPix, Wedibox, Kululu and WedUploader collect existing photos and videos.
They are stronger when completeness, mixed media, later uploads or a Drive
destination matter. Participation may need more reminders because guests can
postpone selecting files.

## Five-step decision

1. Write “we want every file” or “we want a shared camera.”
2. Decide whether video and a live display are required.
3. Choose acceptable guest entry: browser, App Clip or app.
4. Test the full flow on iPhone and Android.
5. Compare the live total, retention and complete download.
