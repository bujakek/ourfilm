A wedding photo scavenger hunt works best with optional prompts, one shared
camera or upload link and no pressure to photograph anyone who objects. Give
guests ideas rather than turning the reception into a compulsory contest.

## Simple rules

Choose ten to fifteen prompts for each table or publish the full list. Guests
scan one QR code, submit their images and optionally add the prompt number.
Set a finish time before the end of the reception and decide whether there will
be prizes, table scoring or no competition at all.

## Privacy and moderation

Never reward intrusive or embarrassing images. Avoid prompts involving kissing,
alcohol, children or personal characteristics unless participation is clearly
voluntary. Let the host hide entries before a public slideshow.

Connect the game to one [wedding photo QR
code](/en/qr-code-for-wedding-photos) so the final collection remains easy to
download.
