Use a wedding hashtag for discoverable public social posts. Use a QR-code album
for private collection, clearer downloads and files that do not depend on one
social feed. The two can coexist if guests understand which destination serves
which purpose.

## Quick comparison

| Question     | Wedding hashtag                  | Private QR album                      |
| ------------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Discovery    | Public and searchable posts      | Direct link for invited guests        |
| Account      | Social-platform account required | Can work without a guest account      |
| File quality | Platform-processed social copy   | Depends on the selected upload tool   |
| Download     | Manual across individual posts   | Host export or ZIP may be available   |
| Privacy      | Public-post settings apply       | Link, PIN or event controls may apply |

## When a hashtag works

A short unique hashtag helps guests find public posts and gives the wedding a
social identity. Search it across relevant platforms before printing, avoid
ambiguous spelling and do not expect private accounts to appear publicly.

## When a QR album works

A QR code can open a private upload page or shared camera. It reaches guests who
do not want to post publicly and can give the couple one managed archive.

## Using both

Write the distinction clearly:

> Private photo upload: scan the QR code.
>
> Public social posts: use #YourWeddingTag.

Download original files from the private route instead of treating the social
feed as the only archive. Start with the [private wedding photo QR
flow](/en/qr-code-for-wedding-photos).
