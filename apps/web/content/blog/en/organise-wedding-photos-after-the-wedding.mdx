**In short:** organise wedding photos by preserving originals first, separating
sources and creating selections from copies. Do not rename, edit and deduplicate
the only version before a verified backup exists.

## 1. Collect every source

Download professional galleries, guest uploads, phone folders, photo-booth
exports, scans, video and audio. Record which source is still incomplete and
its expiry date.

## 2. Verify and back up

Open sample files, extract every ZIP and compare counts. Keep at least one
working copy, one external copy and one off-site or cloud copy.

## 3. Use a simple folder structure

- `01-professional-originals`
- `02-guest-originals`
- `03-video-and-audio`
- `04-favourites`
- `05-edited-exports`
- `06-album-and-print`

Preserve source folders so a later decision can be reversed.

## 4. Handle duplicates carefully

Automatic tools can group visually similar files, but burst images and edited
versions may look like duplicates while carrying different value. Review before
deleting, and keep the highest-quality original plus any intentionally edited
copy.

## 5. Create useful selections

Make a small “share,” “print,” “album” and “family archive” selection rather
than one giant favourites folder. Export social copies separately from
print-ready files.

## 6. Finish one physical result

A printed book, framed group picture or small guest-photo zine makes the
archive easier to revisit. Keep project files with the final exports.
