**In short:** a good DIY wedding photo corner needs more light than props.
Choose a clean background, place a large soft light near eye level, mark where
guests should stand and give them one QR route for saving or sharing the result.

## Choose the location

Use a space near the reception but away from speaker stacks, service doors and
the main walkway. Allow enough depth for group photos and keep cables taped or
covered.

## Build the background

A curtain, simple floral panel, textured wall or venue feature can work. Avoid
tiny repeated patterns and reflective materials. The background should be wide
enough that three or four people do not reveal storage areas at the edges.

## Improve the light

Use a diffused continuous light rather than relying on ceiling fixtures. Test
skin tones and glare after sunset, when the actual ambient light changes. Do
not aim bright lights into the dance floor or dining tables.

## Add phone support

A stable adjustable stand helps group pictures, but keep a person nearby if
equipment could tip. Provide a simple remote only if it pairs reliably and can
be reset between users.

## Keep props selective

Use a few objects connected to the wedding style instead of a box of generic
signs. A chair, frame, flowers or one playful item often photographs better.

Place the [wedding photo QR code](/en/qr-code-for-wedding-photos) beside the
corner and state whether it opens a camera or accepts existing photos.
