The best wedding photo request starts with thanks, asks for a small action and
includes one direct link. Send it the next day while camera rolls are still
fresh, then use one gentle follow-up before closing the collection.

## Next-day message

> Thank you for celebrating with us. We are still smiling about yesterday. If
> you took any photos or short videos, please add your favourites here: [link].
> We would love to see the day from your point of view.

## Short group-chat version

> Thank you for an unforgettable day. Please send your best wedding photos to
> our shared album: [link]. One or two favourites are perfect.

## Message for a QR card or email

> You helped make our wedding what it was. Help us remember the moments we
> missed by adding your photos to our private guest album before [date]: [link].

## Reminder message

> A small final reminder before we close our guest album on [date]. If there is
> a photo from the wedding you think we should keep, please add it here: [link].
> Thank you again for being part of the day.

## For original-quality files

> If you have a photo we may want to print, please upload the original file
> rather than sending a screenshot or social-media copy: [link].

## Make the request easier to answer

Set a deadline, explain whether video is accepted and avoid asking guests to
join several folders. A request for “your best three” feels achievable; “send
everything” can be postponed indefinitely.

The strongest strategy is still to introduce the [guest upload
flow](/en/wedding-guest-photo-upload) during the wedding rather than depending
entirely on messages afterwards.
