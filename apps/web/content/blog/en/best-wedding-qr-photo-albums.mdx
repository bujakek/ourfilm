The phrase “wedding QR photo album” can mean an upload page, a shared guest
camera, a live slideshow or a route into the couple's cloud storage. The best
choice depends on what guests should see immediately after scanning.

## Best category by outcome

- **Shared digital film:** OurFilm, Once, POV or Lense.
- **Photo and video upload gallery:** GuestPix or Wedibox.
- **Live venue wall:** Kululu or Lense.
- **Google Drive destination:** WedUploader.

Product details were reviewed on 2026-09-17. Check current plans and regional
pricing before printing an event code.

## Upload album

Guests select existing photos and videos from their phones. This is strongest
for volume and later contributions. It can be postponed because the guest is
completing an upload task rather than joining an activity.

## Digital guest camera

The QR code opens the event camera, shows a remaining shot count and applies the
host's reveal rules. This creates a consistent shared experience but may not
accept old camera-roll files.

## Slideshow gallery

New uploads appear on a venue display. This adds energy and needs moderation,
stable connectivity and tested playback equipment.

## Drive collection

Files move into storage controlled by the couple. This simplifies long-term
ownership but may provide a less polished event experience unless a gallery
layer is included.

## QR placement checklist

Print the same final code on tables and signs, explain whether it opens a camera
or upload page, state any limit or reveal time, include a fallback URL and test
the physical sample on multiple phones.
