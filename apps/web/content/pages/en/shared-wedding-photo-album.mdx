A shared wedding photo album can be more than a folder waiting for uploads.
With OurFilm, guests make the album together through one QR-code camera, and
every completed shot lands in the same private gallery.

If the host waits until the event ends to reveal the photos, the couple and
their guests can discover the finished film together: one wedding seen from
dozens of different places in the room.

## How guests build the album

1. The host creates the wedding camera and selects a roll length.
2. Guests scan one QR code and enter a name, without an app or account.
3. Each person receives 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 frames.
4. New photos enter the shared private gallery automatically.
5. The host controls guest viewing, hides individual images when needed and
   downloads the complete album.

The professional photographer remains responsible for the polished story of
the day. The shared guest album adds the side conversations, dance-floor
accidents and table-level moments that one photographer cannot see at once.

## Album or wedding film?

The album is the result. The shared camera is the experience that produces it.
Guests work with a finite roll, photograph during the celebration and may not
see the results until later.

That changes the character of the collection. Instead of selecting only the
most polished images from their phone after the wedding, guests contribute the
small, immediate moments they noticed while they were there.

## Private sharing and host control

The album is not publicly listed. Guests reach it through the event's
unguessable QR code or link, and the host decides whether the revealed gallery
is visible to them.

The host can review every photo, hide individual images and download the album
as a ZIP. The link is still shareable by anyone who has it, so it should stay
within the intended wedding group.

## Planning the reveal

Choose immediate reveal when browsing the growing album should be part of the
reception. Choose end-of-event reveal when you want the disposable-camera
surprise and a clear moment to look back together.

Whichever option you use, tell guests what to expect on the QR sign. A short
line such as “Your photos will be developed after the party” prevents them
from assuming an upload failed when no gallery appears immediately.

## What the shared album does not collect

OurFilm currently handles still photographs taken inside its camera. It does
not accept videos, voice messages or bulk uploads from an existing camera roll,
and it does not provide a live slideshow. A general media gallery is a better
choice when those formats are essential.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the wedding film remain private?

Yes. It is unlisted and reached through the event link or QR code. The host can
also keep guest gallery viewing disabled.

### Must the photos appear immediately?

No. They can appear immediately or only after the event ends.

### Can we download every photo together?

Yes. The host can export the complete album after the wedding.
