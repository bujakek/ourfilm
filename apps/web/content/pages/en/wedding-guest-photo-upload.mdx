**In short:** OurFilm starts one step before an upload does — the photo is taken
inside the event camera and reaches the private gallery on its own, so nobody
has to find and send anything afterwards. If what you actually want is your
guests' existing camera-roll files, an upload album or a cloud folder is the
better tool.

Most wedding photo upload tools begin after a picture already exists. OurFilm
starts one step earlier: guests scan a QR code and take the photo inside the
wedding's shared camera, so it reaches the private gallery automatically.

If you only need people to submit hundreds of existing camera-roll files after
the day, a conventional upload album or Google Drive may suit you better.
OurFilm is for couples who want the act of taking guest photos to become part of
the celebration.

## From QR code to private gallery

1. Create the event and choose 5, 10, 16, 24 or 36 shots per guest.
2. Decide whether photos appear immediately or after the event ends.
3. Share the event's QR code or link on signs, table cards or messages.
4. Guests enter a name and use the browser camera. They do not install an app
   or create an account.
5. The finished album can be downloaded in one ZIP by the host.

## Upload folder or digital guest camera?

| You need                          | Better fit                    |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Existing phone photos and videos  | A traditional upload gallery  |
| Unlimited post-event submissions  | A traditional upload gallery  |
| Spontaneous photos during the day | OurFilm's shared guest camera |
| A finite disposable-camera feel   | OurFilm's host-selected roll  |
| A delayed first look              | OurFilm's end-of-event reveal |

Neither approach is universally better. They solve different problems. An
upload folder maximises the number and type of files collected; a guest camera
shapes how new photos are made.

## Why not allow unlimited shots?

The roll is the creative rule, not a technical shortcoming. A finite number of
frames makes each shutter press matter and keeps the wedding from becoming an
endless phone-photo exercise.

The host chooses the same roll length for every participant. The paid event
removes the participant cap, but it does not turn those rolls into unlimited
uploads.

## Placing the QR code

Put the code where a guest naturally has time to scan it: on dinner tables, by
the bar, beside the guestbook and near any photo corner. Include one clear line
explaining that the code opens a camera, not another app to download.

Test the printed code at its real size and from the distance guests will use.
A perfect upload flow cannot repair a QR card that is too small, glossy or
poorly lit.

## What to know before choosing OurFilm

OurFilm currently accepts new still photos captured through the event camera.
It does not accept videos, audio messages or bulk camera-roll uploads, and it
does not provide a live venue slideshow. Those omissions keep the guest flow
focused, but they make a broader upload platform the honest choice for some
weddings.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can guests upload photos they took earlier on their phone?

No. OurFilm is built around new photos taken inside the shared event camera.

### Does the camera need an internet connection?

Yes. The guest flow and upload queue need network access to deliver the photos
to the event.

### Can the host download the complete album?

Yes. The event's photos can be downloaded together after the wedding.
